Our Story

Since 1995

Three decades of summers 💛

How it started

The Ice Hut has been family-owned and operated in the heart of Morinville since 1995. What started as a little hut with a big freezer has become the place where first dates, little-league wins, and long summer evenings all end the same way: with an enormous cone.

We keep it simple: premium Alberta-made Foothills Creamery ice cream, scooped generously, served with a smile. We source locally where we can (hello, Haskap berries) and love supporting neighbourhood events.
