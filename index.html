A Morinville tradition, one scoop at a time 💛

Three decades of summers

The Ice Hut has been family-owned and operated in the heart of Morinville since 1995. What started as a little hut with a big freezer has become the place where first dates, little-league wins, and long summer evenings all end the same way: with an enormous cone.

We keep it simple: premium Alberta-made Foothills Creamery ice cream, scooped generously, served with a smile. We source locally where we can (hello, Haskap berries) and love supporting neighbourhood events.

Good to know

Questions? Scooped. 🍧

Where is The Ice Hut located?

We're at 9911 100 Street, Morinville, Alberta, T8R 1R4, right in the heart of town (about 20 minutes north of Edmonton).

What are your hours?

We scoop May through September! May–June: Mon–Fri 3:00pm–9:30pm, weekends 12:00pm–9:30pm. July–August: every day 12:00pm–9:30pm. September: Mon–Fri 3:00pm–9:00pm, weekends 12:00pm–9:00pm. Closed for the season October–April.

What kind of ice cream do you serve?

Premium Foothills Creamery ice cream, an Alberta favourite, with 75+ rotating flavours: from timeless classics to fan favourites like Sea Salt Caramel Fudge and New York Cherry Cheesecake. Sorbets, sherbets, lactose-free and no-sugar-added options too.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Yes! Our flavour board flags no-nuts, no-gluten, no-eggs, no-lactose and no-dairy options, we carry sorbets and no-sugar-added flavours, and you can even get a gluten-free cone for $1.50. Just ask at the window.

Can I bring my dog?

Absolutely, four-legged customers are some of our favourites. Treat them to a Puppy Cone for $2.25 and watch the tail go.

How long has The Ice Hut been around?

We've been family-owned and scooping in Morinville since 1995, over three decades of summers and counting.
