Three decades of summers

The Ice Hut has been family-owned and operated in the heart of Morinville since 1995. What started as a little hut with a big freezer has become the place where first dates, little-league wins, and long summer evenings all end the same way: with an enormous cone.

We keep it simple — premium Alberta-made Foothills Creamery ice cream, scooped generously, served with a smile.

Good to know

Questions? Scooped. 🍧

Where is The Ice Hut located?

We're at 9911 100 Street, Morinville, Alberta, T8R 1R4 — right in the heart of town, about 20 minutes north of Edmonton.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday we're open 4:00pm–9:00pm, and on weekends (Saturday & Sunday) we open early at 12:00pm and scoop until 9:00pm.

What kind of ice cream do you serve?

Premium Foothills Creamery ice cream — an Alberta favourite — with 48 rotating flavours, from timeless classics to new fan favourites like salted caramel and New York cherry cheesecake.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Yes! Many of our flavours are gluten-free, and our team is happy to help you find a treat that fits your dietary needs. Just ask at the window.

Can I bring my dog?

Absolutely — four-legged customers are some of our favourites. Treat them to a Puppy Cone for $2.25 and watch the tail go.

How long has The Ice Hut been around?

We've been family-owned and scooping in Morinville since 1995 — over three decades of summers and counting.
