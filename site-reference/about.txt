The Ice Hut Story

Welcome to The Ice Hut, a cherished family-owned ice cream oasis nestled in the heart of our community. With a tradition spanning generations, we've been dishing out happiness, one scoop at a time, since 1997. What sets us apart is not just our creamy, handcrafted flavors made from the finest ingredients, but also the warmth of our hospitality and the joyous memories we help create.

At The Ice Hut, every visit is a journey back to simpler times, where families gather, friends reunite, and laughter fills the air. From classic favorites like velvety vanilla and rich chocolate to inventive seasonal delights bursting with fresh fruits and whimsical toppings, there's a flavor to delight every palate.

But beyond the ice cream, it's our commitment to family values and community that truly defines us. We source locally whenever possible, support neighborhood events, and cherish the relationships we've built over the years. When you step up to The Ice Hut, you're not just a customer – you're part of our extended family.

So whether you're cooling off on a hot summer day or catching up with a friend after a long hard day, join us at The Ice Hut for a taste of tradition, a sprinkle of happiness, and a whole lot of joy.
