Questions? Scooped. 🍧

Everything people ask us most, in one place.

Where is The Ice Hut located?

The Ice Hut is at 9911 100 Street, Morinville, Alberta, T8R 1R4, right in the heart of town (about 20 minutes north of Edmonton).

What are The Ice Hut's hours?

We scoop May through September. May–June: Monday–Friday 3:00pm–9:30pm, weekends 12:00pm–9:30pm. July–August: every day 12:00pm–9:30pm. September: Monday–Friday 3:00pm–9:00pm, weekends 12:00pm–9:00pm. Closed October–April.

What kind of ice cream does The Ice Hut serve?

We scoop premium Foothills Creamery ice cream, an Alberta favourite, with 75+ rotating flavours: from classics like Chocolate and Tiger to Sea Salt Caramel Fudge and New York Cherry Cheesecake.

Does The Ice Hut have gluten-free options?

Yes. Our flavour board tags every flavour that contains gluten, and gluten-free cones are available for $1.50. There are also dairy-free sorbets, lactose-free and no-sugar-added flavours. Gluten isn't separated the way nuts are, so please tell staff about a serious intolerance and we'll check the tub's ingredient list with you.

Which flavours contain nuts?

Twelve: All Canadian Moose, Belgian Hazelnut, Butter Pecan, Caramel Crunch – NSA, Chocolate Peanut Butter, Funky Monkey, Kulfi, Maple Nut, Peanut Butter Crunch, Pistachio with Almonds, Rocky Road and Turtle Mountain. Each one is marked Contains Nuts on the flavour board.

Those twelve live in a separate freezer with their own scoops, so they're never scooped with the utensils used on the rest of the board.

A flavour without the tag still isn't certified nut-free — cones, toppings and the milkshake blender are shared. If you have a nut allergy, please tell us at the window before you order: we'll check the tub's ingredient list with you and make up your order accordingly.

Can I bring my dog to The Ice Hut?

Absolutely, four-legged friends are welcome, and we serve a Puppy Cone made just for them for $2.25.

How long has The Ice Hut been open?

The Ice Hut has been family-owned and scooping in Morinville since 1995, over three decades of summers.

How much is a milkshake at The Ice Hut?

Milkshakes are $9.00 for 24oz or $11.00 for 32oz, in any flavour.

Does The Ice Hut have outdoor seating?

Yes, there's a grassy area with picnic benches right at the Hut, perfect for enjoying a cone outside on a summer evening.

What payment methods does The Ice Hut accept?

The Ice Hut accepts cash, credit card, and debit card.
